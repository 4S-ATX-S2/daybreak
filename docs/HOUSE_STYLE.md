# 03 — HOUSE STYLE

## Voice
Written to **inform, not alarm.** Plain, warm, hotel-staff register — not police-blotter, not corporate.
Every section answers *"so what does this mean for us today?"* Say the uncertain thing out loud rather
than smoothing it. **The brief's credibility comes from what it refuses to assert.**

Signature moves worth reusing:
- Name the discrepancy rather than resolving it.
- "Unknown, not fine" — a reporting gap is a blind spot, not a clean bill of health.
- Publish the trigger *before* it fires ("Red holds unless the index verifies at 102° or below") —
  then when it fires, the step is a rule, not a judgement.
- Anchor The Craft in a real, local, **de-identified** incident from that morning's own releases.

## FULL — 12 sections (fixed order)
01 The Day Ahead (lead + TODAY'S FOCUS + "Since Yesterday" strip) · 02 Conditions at a Glance ·
03 Weather & Comfort · 04 Around the Neighborhood · 05 Gatherings & City Events (+ map) ·
06 Watch Items · 07 Guest Guidance · 08 Team Notes · **The Craft** · 09 Looking Ahead ·
10 Wider Watch · 11 Emergency Contacts · 12 Sources & Standards (+ confidence note)

## DBS one-pager — fixed blocks
masthead → 5-chip band → lead + focus → **Day at a Glance** (JS day-arc chart + 3 outlook cards) →
**What Today Asks of You** (8 division cells) → Watch Items (3 rows) →
Around the Property + flag | If a Guest Asks (4 Q&A) → The Craft → Readiness → contacts strip → footer

**The 8 divisions, fixed order:** Front Office & Concierge · Guest Services (Valet & Front Drive) ·
Housekeeping · Food & Beverage · Banquets · Engineering · Spa & Fitness · Security & Overnight MOD.
*If a cell says normal, the day is normal — not the brief incomplete.*

## Chips — Calm · Attentive · Elevated · High · Severe
Five areas: Neighborhood & Street · Public Gatherings · Weather & Storms · Traffic & Arrivals · Utilities & Grid.
Trend arrows ▲▼▬. **A chip can hold while its reason changes — say so.**
*First **High** was ed. 249: Gatherings + Traffic, on a UT home opener plus a festival.*

## Exterior Team Flag — from the forecast heat index
| Flag | Condition |
|---|---|
| Green | normal |
| Yellow | index 91–102 |
| **Red** | **index 103–115** |
| Black | heat advisory/warning in effect, or index 115+ |
Read the flag from the **rendered badge**, never from prose (prose legitimately says "flag goes Black"
as a conditional). **Red does NOT reopen WI-09** — a band reading is not an advisory.

## The Craft — rotation
Arc so far: observation → rapport → recovery → de-escalation → welfare → observation →
de-escalation (247) → recovery (248) → **rapport (249)**. **Ed. 250: welfare or observation.**
One verifiable quote per edition, never reused. Used: Jefferson, Carnegie, Sherlock, Proverbs 15:1,
Danny Meyer, Mary Oliver, Seneca, MacDonald, Yogi Berra, Gwendolyn Brooks, Maya Angelou, Rumi,
William James, Simone Weil, JFK, Ambrose Bierce, George Eliot, Lincoln, Publilius Syrus, Samuel Johnson.
**Verify attribution — avoid the popular misattributions (Roosevelt "nobody cares how much you know",
Yeats "no strangers here"). Both are apocryphal.**

## Readiness — 12-topic rotation
Topic 2 Medical Response & AED · Topic 3 Severe Weather & Shelter Points · **Topic 4 Crowds, Capacity
& Getting People Out (ed. 249)** · **Ed. 250 takes Topic 5.**
Three columns always: **KNOW THIS · DO THIS TODAY · IF IT HAPPENS**, each ending in a memorable
three-beat ("call, AED, door" · "inside, away from glass, thirty minutes" · "open a route, name it, split the flow").

## Watch Items
`WI-NN`, statuses: New · Open · Monitoring · Action · Closing · Closed.
**Age = prior edition's age + days elapsed.** Never recompute from the first-noted date mid-item.
Each entry ends with **Next check** and a bold **property action** (or "no property action").
A closed item drops off the register the following edition.

## Retired framings — never reintroduce
- The Strait of Hormuz is **never** "closed" or "shut" in our own voice.
- The tracker's **day-count metric** is retired.
- Exception: text that quotes them *in order to forbid them* ("do not tell a guest the strait is closed").
