## The eight divisions — FIXED ORDER, confirmed by Ryan
Front Office & Concierge · **Guest Services (Valet & Front Drive)** · Housekeeping · Food & Beverage · **Banquets** · Engineering · **Spa & Fitness** (pool deck lives here) · Security & Overnight MOD.
Banquets, Spa & Fitness, and Guest Services are **individual divisions** — do not merge them. Keep the order stable edition to edition; people learn where their cell is. **Never leave a cell empty** — write "Nothing specific — normal standards."
⚠️ **When a Watch Item is cut from the DBS for page-fit, make sure its property action survives in the relevant division cell.** Ed. 247 dropped WI-10 and WI-11 to the FULL and kept "exterior walk for standing water" in Security and the Gulf-hub flag in Front Office.

## What was CUT from the security 1PG to pay for the additions
- **"Since Yesterday" strip — deleted entirely.** A continuity device for a reader who read yesterday's brief; a DBS reader dips in. Biggest single saving.
- **Watch Items collapsed from 4 dense paragraphs to 4 one-line rows.** Reasoning, sourcing and metric history stay in the FULL. **Every row ends with a bold gold action clause — if you cannot write one, the item does not belong on the DBS page.**
- **Geopolitics demoted.** Oil to the cent, vessel counts, transit baselines, tracker probability horizons are security-office instrumentation, not division information. Keep only the clause that touches this address. (Ed. 247 kept "an independent count of four tanker crossings" but dropped the source name "Kpler" — right call for this audience.)
- **"Next 72 Hours" text boxes → the day-arc chart + 3 compact outlook cards.**
- **Contacts table → one horizontal strip.**

## Content rules for the wider audience
- **No case numbers, no suspect descriptions, no investigative detail.** Direction and distance only.
- Nothing on the page should need a security background to act on.
- If a line does not change what somebody DOES today, cut it.

## The day-arc chart
Generated by a script at the foot of the file from a `DAY` object: `hours`, `air`, `index`, `rain` ([start,end] decimal hours or null), `sun` ([sunrise,sunset] decimal hours). Edit six values, everything redraws — bands, gridlines, hour axis, sunrise/sunset markers, peak callout. Series: **house gold #94803a dashed = air temp**, **flag red #a4331f solid = heat index**; the pair passes CVD separation (ΔE 11.0 deutan, 17.8 normal) with dash + direct labels as secondary encoding. Callouts carry a white halo (`paint-order:stroke`) so they can sit over a line or a band.
⚠️ **The chart is drawn by JavaScript — it does not exist in the static HTML.** Any converter that does not run scripts (LibreOffice, pandoc) produces an empty box. See the DOCX recipe in [[ed247-state]].

## Page-fit
Placeholder edition rendered 1 page at 93% ink. **Cut order when overrunning:** 4th watch item → 4th guest Q&A → 2nd "Around the Property" bullet → the map. **Never cut Readiness or the by-division row.** Follow the *spirit* over the letter: ed. 247 cut the lowest-value Q&A (the storm one, once that item closed) rather than mechanically the fourth.
⚠️ **THE `.qa` TRAP — the most valuable page-fit lesson so far.** `.qa .q` and `.qa .a` are hard-coded at **7.9px** in the screen CSS with **no print override**, so shrinking `body` in `@media print` never touches the entire "If a Guest Asks" column — type cuts appear to do nothing, and the left column ends up visibly smaller than the right. **Always add a print rule for `.qa`.** Ed. 247: `.qa .q{font-size:6.6px;} .qa .a{font-size:6.6px;line-height:1.26;margin:0 0 2.6px;}` — that freed enough to raise `body` from 6.08 to **6.85px**, ending *more* readable than it started and still 1pp at 97%.
⚠️ **The header-reclaim lever still works** (`.masthead{padding:0}`, h1 to 18.5px, `.band{margin:0.5px 0}`).
⚠️ The field marker `.f` is a soft gold wash (`rgba(148,128,58,.135)`), NOT a dotted underline — the underline repeated on every wrapped line and looked like ruled paper. **Never put `.f` on an element that already carries a status colour** (chips, badges, the heat-flag `.lvl`) — it washes them out.
⚠️ The 1PG's `.flag .lvl` hardcodes `background:var(--flag-black)` — **always set the right token inline**, e.g. `style="background:var(--flag-red)"`.
⚠️ Map labels need font sizes ~50% larger than the FULL's, because the map renders at ~3.4 in wide in a column. **The map has been cut on both live editions so far (246, 247)** — it is the first thing to go and nothing has missed it.

## Readiness — the 12-topic rotation
1 Fire alarm & evacuation *(ed. 246)* · 2 Medical response & AED *(ed. 247)* · **3 Severe weather & shelter — next, ed. 248** · 4 Run–Hide–Fight · 5 Suspicious person/package · 6 Elevator entrapment · 7 Power failure & emergency lighting · 8 Water intrusion & flooding · 9 Guest in crisis / welfare check · 10 Bomb-threat call procedure · 11 Kitchen fire & suppression · 12 Missing child / lost guest.
Fixed three-column structure: **Know This** (the fact) · **Do This Today** (one action, this shift) · **If It Happens** (first move, then who to call — hold it to the first thirty seconds). A real lesson learned **replaces** the rotation topic; de-identify completely (no names, no room numbers, no guest details).
**What makes these land:** one hard number the reader will remember (10% per minute), one physical action they can do today in two minutes, and one named failure mode ("do not send *someone*" — point and name). Tie to an external hook where one exists — ed. 247 used the City's National Preparedness Month kickoff.
