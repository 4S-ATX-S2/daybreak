## Verified facts used (retrieved 30 Aug 2026)
- **Tex. Occ. Code §1702.104** — investigations company license required for furnishing info about "crime or wrongs done or threatened against a person" and a person's "movement, location, affiliations, associations," for hire. **§1702.102** — separate license for a "private security consultant company."
- **§1702.323** — exempts an individual employed "in an employee-employer relationship exclusively and regularly by one employer" in connection with employer affairs; **"the security department of a private business is not required to apply for any license under this chapter."** §1702.323(d) withdraws it in defined circumstances. **This exemption is the whole reason the hotel-owned model was recommended** — a Rowan-as-vendor sale sits squarely inside the licensing regime.
- **Negligent undertaking** — *Torrington Co. v. Stutzman*, 46 S.W.3d 829, 837 (Tex. 2000). Charging doesn't create the duty but makes reliance easy to establish.
- **TDPSA** eff. 1 Jul 2024; SBA small-business exemption (FS Austin doesn't qualify); AG enforces at **$7,500/violation**, 30-day cure.
- EP rates **$1,200–2,500/day, $80–150/hr** (Dahlcore guide, updated 17 Mar 2026). Global Rescue individual **from $139/yr**, destination reports **215 countries** — the "generic vs local" foil.
