## ⚠️⚠️ TWO PIPELINE LESSONS WORTH MORE THAN THE EDITION
**1. The `.qa` trap GENERALISES — it was never just `.qa`.** On the DBS, **almost every block hard-codes its font-size in the SCREEN css** (`.lead`, `.lead .focus`, `.craft .cl`, `.craft .q`, `.wi .r`, `.dv p`, `.out .d p`, `.ready p`, `.flag .t`, `.cell .lab/.chip/.tr`, `h3.dash`). **`body{font-size}` in print therefore moves almost nothing** — a 6.85→6.55px cut bought only ~0.67in. **Adding explicit print overrides for every one of those selectors took the DBS from 2pp to 1pp AND let the type be raised back up.** *Rule: when a print page overflows, grep the screen CSS for every hard-coded `font-size` in the overflowing region before touching `body`.*
**2. `tblLayout:fixed` is ignored unless `w:tblGrid` is rewritten.** `cell.width` + `autofit=False` alone does nothing — Word and LibreOffice lay out from `w:tblGrid`. `fix_widths()` clears and rebuilds the gridCols and sets `w:tblW`, and honours the `width:NN%` on the source `<th>`s. **This recovered enough dead vertical space that the FULL went from needing scale 0.86 to fitting at ~1.0–1.07.** Also: **on tables of ≥6 columns never shade the status cell** — Word stretches the fill to full row height and a tall WI row becomes a black slab. Colour the badge *text*; shade only narrow/short tables.
**3. The map is the FULL's packing lever, as ed. 247 documented.** The review-pass corrections pushed the FULL to 6pp; **taking the map from 68% to 58% width brought it back to 5pp** with no prose cut. Never crop the viewBox — change the width percentage.
